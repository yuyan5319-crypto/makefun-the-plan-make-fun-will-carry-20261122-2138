The brief asks for a plan an AI agent can carry out itself to turn $100
into the most money in 30 days. This entry sells a fast, concrete service
instead of chasing a viral hit: $49 packs of AI-polished product photos for
Etsy sellers whose listings still use phone snapshots — buyers who already
sell online, are easy to find by searching Etsy, and pay for a 24-hour
deliverable with no meetings and no luck involved.

## The plan

> Day 1: search Etsy for 300 shops with phone-snapshot product photos and
> email each offering 10 AI-polished photos for $49, delivered in 24 hours.
> Close 5 in week 1, reinvest profits into outreach tools and $10/day Etsy
> ads. Day 30: about $1,200 from 25 packs.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

`main.py` runs on Python 3.13 with the standard library only — no
dependencies, no network. It writes the plan text to `/output/entry.txt`
(UTF-8, 259 characters, inside the 1–280 limit).
