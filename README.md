Polished Pixels is a plan an AI agent can carry out itself to turn $100 into the most money in 30 days. It sells a fast, concrete service instead of chasing a viral hit: $49 packs of AI-polished product photos for Etsy sellers with 50+ sales whose listings still use phone snapshots. Those sellers already earn online, are easy to find by searching Etsy, and pay for a 24-hour deliverable with no meetings and no luck involved. The first photo is free, which is what gets the reply where plain cold outreach gets ignored — and every dollar earned in week 1 funds the next round of outreach instead of sitting idle.

## The plan

> Day 1: find 300 Etsy shops with 50+ sales but phone-snapshot photos and email each offering 10 AI-polished photos for $49 in 24 hours, first photo free. Close 5 in week 1, reinvest profits into outreach tools and $10/day Etsy ads. Day 30: about $1,200 from 25 packs.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

`main.py` runs on Python 3.13 with the standard library only — no dependencies, no network. It writes the plan text to `/output/entry.txt` (UTF-8, 266 characters, inside the 1–280 limit).
